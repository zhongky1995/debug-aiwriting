# Social Content Register

Use for finished social posts, 小红书图文/笔记/硬广, carousels, and feed ads. Use marketing-strategy-register.md for a campaign plan and ugc-persona-script-register.md for an actual speaker or audiovisual script. A credible social post can be emotional, practical, commercial, analytical, or narrative; it need not imitate a personal experience.

These are editorial criteria, not verified platform rules or a promise of reach or conversion. Use supplied evidence for audience and product claims. Check current platform requirements through an authorized source only when they matter to the task.

## Basis And Evidence Limits

This reference was initially constructed from a local writing-failure audit and editorial reasoning. The audit exposed repeated advice-shaped progression and revisions driven by length and price placement; it did not establish why particular readers disengaged. Treat the proposed repairs as editorial hypotheses, not experimentally validated platform advice.

Public sources checked on 2026-10-15 provide limited contextual support, added after the initial draft:

- [Xiaohongshu Community Convention 2.0](https://pgy.xiaohongshu.com/help/detail?id=1eda0a065dd894063c2e029a49e8f6a1&userType=4): the official text supports truthful material, title/content alignment, and substantiated product/service descriptions. These are content boundaries, not evidence of reach or conversion.
- [Burnkrant and Unnava, 1995, Effects of Self-Referencing on Persuasion](https://academic.oup.com/jcr/article-abstract/22/1/17/1808102?login=false): the publisher's abstract reports two experiments in which self-reference increased elaboration and could improve persuasion with strong arguments; other elaboration-enhancing conditions moderated or reversed the benefit. This supports considering reader relevance alongside argument quality, not assuming that more emotional identification always helps.
- [Berger and Milkman, 2012, What Makes Online Content Viral?](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1509/jmr.10.0353): the publisher's abstract describes a three-month New York Times article study and experiments. Practical usefulness, interest, and surprise were positively associated with sharing; emotional effects depended on activation, with sadness performing differently from high-arousal emotions. Sharing of news articles does not establish feed attention or purchases for Chinese listening-service ads.

The public-source check used accessible publisher abstracts and indexed official text; it was not a full-paper review or a platform experiment. These sources do not validate a fixed carousel sequence, an ideal word count, a price frequency, a requirement for a memorable line, or the claim that every emotional post should omit advice. Those choices still require judgment against the brief and actual reader evidence.

## Choose The Content Job

Infer the dominant job from the brief and complaint; keep supporting jobs subordinate. Keep this working choice internal unless the user asks for analysis.

| Job | What earns attention or continuation | Common failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emotional recognition / 痛点共鸣 | A precise friction, unsaid thought, or contradiction that the intended reader recognizes; progression reveals what the opening did not yet name | A broad pain label followed immediately by reassurance or advice |
| Useful / saveable | A specific question, discriminating example, comparison, or usable action with relevant conditions | Three obvious tips decorated as new insight |
| Product / hard ad | A real need or buying hesitation developed through what the offer provides, why that matters here, and how to consider it | A feature/process list whose relevance the reader must supply; a long story with a generic sales tail |
| Viewpoint / discussion | A defensible position and the observation or reasoning that makes it worth discussing | An absolute claim for drama, or a balanced paragraph that says nothing |
| Story / experience | A supported or explicitly authorized fictional event, meaningful change, and a payoff | Fake first-person testimony or a moral added after the story ends |

Mixed content still needs one center of gravity. In a product hard ad, develop the offer and its use in the body; any opening pain should make that offer relevant. In a pain-led ad, recognition carries more of the body, with a service connection that follows from that specific difficulty. A personal story is one possible form when appropriate, not the default for a platform. These are choices of emphasis, not fixed paragraph sequences. A request for ads remains commercial even when the writing gains an attractive observation or a complete story.

## Find An Angle Worth Reading

A subject names the area; an angle selects what is interesting within it. “边界感” names an area. “拒绝的话没写完，已经道歉了三次” selects a recognizable action and an unresolved difficulty.

Find the relevant friction in the source: what the person tries to do, what interferes, and the consequence or meaning they struggle to articulate. Choose a position close enough to that experience to feel accurate. Professional qualification does not require the distant stance of someone explaining the reader to themselves.

Choose a speaking position suited to the account and purpose. A personal voice can carry a composed public-facing post; a brand need not imitate a customer's diary. Decide what this reader should recognize, understand, or consider, then use [whole-artifact composition](whole-artifact-writing.md#integrated-composition-at-the-requested-length) to select and organize the available material. An experience can support a point without being retold from beginning to end. Keep a genuine difference between what a paragraph contributes and what the preceding paragraph already made clear.

Use connected, natural Chinese and preserve necessary triggers, responses, and reasons. Dialogue, action, feeling, observation, and reflection can share the work. Give each the space its role needs; do not fix imbalance by counting pronouns or deleting all interiority. A supported reflection can make the experience worth sharing without becoming advice or a universal diagnosis. A scene can convey that significance without an explicit moral. When a draft reads like a private log, inspect selection, emphasis, progression, and closure before changing person or adding headings. Create illustrative events only within creative authorization; repair nonfiction from its evidence.

Use the weakest adequate claim. A close observation or a proposed way to phrase the difficulty can be incisive without diagnosing personality, assigning another person's motive, or claiming prevalence. Do not replace editorial conviction with a chain of “可能、如果、也许、可以先”; retain a qualification when it changes the truth or the decision.

Before expanding, identify the interesting movement. It might expose a contradiction, sharpen an observation, answer an uncertainty, compare choices, or show a consequence. “场景 → 共鸣 → 建议 → 私信” describes slots, not a movement.

## Title, Images, Body, And Ending

The title and first image make a promise the content can fulfill: what situation, question, benefit, or tension is worth the reader's next moment. Not every title needs suspense, a question, an emotional extreme, or a slogan. Reject a hook whose force depends on an unsupported accusation or a benefit the product cannot deliver.

For a carousel, judge the image sequence as a reading experience. The next image should reveal something useful to that specific post: an implication, a contrast, a telling detail, a worked example, a product answer, or a meaningful turn. Rewording the previous image is not progress. A paragraph of sound prose split across four images is not automatically a good carousel.

Let the content determine page roles within the user's specified count. A visual template may handle fonts and spacing; it must not require every middle image to contain three tips or every ending to contain a summary quote. Repeated branding can remain consistent while the information moves differently.

The body must make sense with its title. When both body and images are requested, decide what each contributes; the body may deepen, give context, explain relevant conditions, or state the offer. Essential facts can repeat where needed for independent reading, but avoid publishing two identical essays. Caption length, emoji count, and paragraph count are not measures of social fluency.

The ending should deliver the chosen job: a recognition the reader can carry, an answer, an unresolved but sharper question, a decision aid, a narrative beat, or a relevant offer. A complete ending need not restate the opening or dissolve the tension with reassurance. An invitation to act should be clear enough to use without overwhelming the content.

## Commercial Integration And Evidence

Apply the [Material Sufficiency Gate](core-quality-gates.md#material-sufficiency-gate) to the chosen buying question. Where the source supplies relevant service actions, connect them to what the reader can use or decide; repeating service labels or reassurance leaves that material unused. Where the source only names “梳理、支持、陪伴”, do not expand those labels into unconfirmed procedures, deliverables, or results. Write the strongest copy the known facts support; if a missing product definition materially limits the task, identify the specific gap outside the copy. Make a needed connection in the body; a stronger title or closing invitation cannot supply it. Where the use is evident, leave it implicit rather than adding a benefit sentence after every feature.

Compare two illustrative briefs for the same service. “30分钟简历梳理” supports the duration and topic, but does not establish line-by-line rewriting, a finished résumé, or improved interview rates. If the brief also supplies “对照目标岗位，标出相关经历与未写清的证据，并提供修改批注”, the copy can explain how those annotations help the reader select and supplement résumé content. Still writing only “帮你梳理简历” would then omit useful known material. Borrow this distinction, not the example's facts or wording. Do not invent service procedures, qualifications, availability, privacy promises, testimonials, or outcomes to strengthen the offer.

Use price where it helps the immediate buying decision and fits the brief. Repetition may damage emphasis; removing price everywhere can also remove a useful reason to consider a hard ad. “Too much price” calls for an information hierarchy, not a universal quota. Keep other explicit sales and CTA requirements unless the user changes them.

Put a boundary next to the claim it limits when necessary. Do not let every emotional passage become a disclaimer or let a product introduction consist mainly of things the service cannot do. Stronger content comes from selecting a supported positive reason to consider the offer, not from stronger promises.

Authorized original social copy may dramatize a supplied audience pattern with illustrative wording or dialogue. Keep its status clear in the surrounding presentation; do not attribute it to an actual customer, fabricate a personal experience, or present it as an observed case. A possible scene does not establish a psychological cause, typicality, or an effect of the service.

When a fictional first-person persona is explicitly authorized, label that status in the review context and keep the voice and knowledge consistent. A personal-story demonstration is not automatically a publishable customer testimonial. A real brand writer must speak from supported knowledge; do not invent counseling cases or professional experience. If an ad is required, integrate the offer from that speaker's actual position and need rather than switching abruptly to an omniscient brand narrator at the end.

## Read References For Function

When the user asks for excellent/popular platform style or rejects an uncompetitive draft, calibrate against actual relevant posts before deriving more rules or offering another internally justified sample. Record what is accessible: title, body and image text, author/account context when available, visible engagement counts and observation date, and content type. A caption alone does not establish a carousel's writing style; a popular video without a readable transcript is not a prose benchmark. Inspect available comments for what readers actually respond to, without equating engagement with sales or causation.

Choose references for demonstrated writing quality as well as visible response and fit to this audience. Keep low-engagement client references separate from high-response examples. If direct access is limited, use accessible original posts or traceable snapshots and describe the limitation; generic viral-writing tutorials, unverified performance claims, and literature alone cannot replace the requested style calibration.

Compare specific language decisions: speaker stance and proximity, lexical character, sentence movement, emotional progression, concrete contrasts, and the ending's effect. Preserve distinctive useful choices across the whole rewrite. “More concrete”, “shorter”, or classifying a sample as emotional/useful/product does not defend flat copy. Brief recognition can work, but an unresolved relationship conflict may need sustained development; do not treat brevity or an early quiet ending as the default cure.

Use actual available examples, not an imagined universal “小红书风格”. Distinguish what is visible from what is inferred: the opening action, chosen tension, information reveal, image/body relationship, product placement, and the role of the ending. Explain why a useful choice fits this brief before borrowing it. An example's length, repeated price, or warm tone may be incidental.

An account or note provided as a reference does not establish that its format performed well. Engagement counts, if available, also do not by themselves prove a causal mechanism or paid conversion. If only screenshots or a stored excerpt are accessible, calibrate to that evidence and state the limit when material.

For category education and emotional recognition, a topic can legitimately be relevant to multiple brands. Judge its audience precision, useful position, and truthful reason for this account to publish it. Require product-specific support for a differentiation claim; do not force an exclusive brand advantage into an ordinary human concern.

## Batch Work And Feedback

For a batch, compare the substance of angles and reader gains, not just title wording, photos, or layout names. Related topics can coexist when they do different work. “脑子很乱怎么开口” and “一次倾诉如何选话题” need distinguishable answers; changing the label is not enough.

Before scaling a coupled revision, resolve representative complete posts that differ in function, then inspect the whole batch for repeated progression. This is an authoring check, not a required extra approval or automatic independent-agent task. If all posts collapse to “先梳理几个问题，再私信”, revise the underlying content decisions. Keep the requested quantity; disclose a specific material gap if distinct work cannot be supported.

Interpret complaints as evidence about the reader experience:

- “太短、没感染力”: look for undeveloped tension, an absent payoff, or a weak angle before adding words.
- “又长又臭”: look for repeated recognition, unnecessary qualification, restated images, or competing jobs before cutting connective meaning.
- “价格太多”: repair emphasis and placement while preserving the commercial job.
- “没头没尾”: check the current task and unmet reader question using [purpose-relative completeness](whole-artifact-writing.md#professional-work-before-prose), before extending the story or adding a pitch.
- “没社媒 sense”: judge audience recognition, continuation, payoff, and the credibility of the offer before adding slang, emojis, or dramatic claims.

Use correction-propagation.md when changes affect analogous posts. Compare the complete candidate with the previous viable draft, including what happened to its force and reader proximity.

## Editorial Acceptance

Read the actual finished surfaces without the planning notes. Explain privately and concretely:

- What would make this intended reader pause here?
- What does the next passage or image add that the opening has not already delivered?
- What can the reader recognize, remember, use, question, or decide at the end?
- For an ad, compare the actual passages with the source using [Commercial Integration And Evidence](#commercial-integration-and-evidence). If value remains unclear, locate unused service facts, a missing product definition, or an unexplained connection rather than merely reporting “卖点不够强”. If the chosen buying question is answered, allow a short piece to finish without explaining every feature; still assess its appeal through the questions above.

Answers that could be pasted onto most posts are insufficient. Name the actual passage that earns the effect, and the weakest point where a reader might leave. Revise that point within scope; do not treat this imagined reader review as measured user behavior.

Accuracy, continuity, clean layout, and coverage are baseline checks. They do not prove appeal, genuine emotional resonance, or commercial results. An editor can judge a candidate's strengths and weaknesses; audience performance remains untested until actual evidence exists.
