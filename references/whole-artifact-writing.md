# Whole-Artifact Writing

Use for generation, authorized structural repair, short texts with competing requirements, and revisions that keep changing without converging. For a local correction, keep only the relevant whole-work check; do not require a planning document or extra approval. This reference adds editorial judgment to the primary genre reference, not a universal article template.

## Current Writing Basis

Keep a compact working note when several decisions interact:

- **Reader outcome:** the understanding, decision, action, or experience this artifact must produce; the reader's relevant starting knowledge.
- **Protected information:** indispensable facts, relationships, uncertainty, commitments, or narrative knowledge boundaries. Distinguish required content from merely available material.
- **Tradeoff order:** which requirement prevails when brevity, completeness, warmth, force, accuracy, or suspense conflict. Follow the user's priorities within evidence and scope constraints.
- **Organizing approach:** how the whole will work at the target length: an answer supported by a mechanism, a comparison enabling a decision, an executable sequence, or a scene changing a relationship. Describe dependencies, not just section titles.

Use information already supplied; ask only for consequential missing information that cannot be responsibly inferred. Keep the note internal by default. Across turns or context handoffs, retain the current basis, viable draft, and unresolved issue; obsolete alternatives should not keep directing the writing.

Facts and purpose constrain the plan, but the initial plan is revisable. New evidence may overturn the working claim. An explicit change of audience or objective updates the basis; a request for a warmer tone usually changes expression while preserving commitments. Do not use the basis to overrule the user's new intent.

## Professional Work Before Prose

Identify the communicative job, not just the publication channel. An article may explain, argue, document a case, teach, or narrate; these jobs need different decisions. For mixed jobs choose the dominant reader outcome and make supporting functions serve it.

The following are checks on decisions, not compulsory sections or public deliverables:

| Function | Decisions needed before or during drafting | Evidence the job is not yet done |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explanation / argument | Specific question; provisional answer and scope; premises, evidence and relevant alternatives; order in which the reader can understand them | Only a topic or attitude; category headings; unsupported causal leap; examples treated as proof |
| Case / retrospective | What changed; relevant actions and observations; what relationship the evidence supports; limits on transfer | Background–challenge–solution labels without a demonstrated relationship; chronology claimed as causality |
| Decision brief / proposal | Decision to make; options or proposed mechanism; evidence, costs and conditions that affect the choice | A service list or recommendation whose advantage cannot be assessed |
| Instruction / SOP | Reader's starting state; executable actions and dependencies; observable completion and relevant failure handling | Accurate individual steps that cannot complete the task together |
| Notice / short copy | Immediate reader question; essential facts and expectation; permissible promise; emphasis under the space limit | Separate sentences for each abstract requirement; warmth replacing useful information; brevity deleting a commitment |
| Narrative / scene | Viewpoint and knowledge; pressure, choice, consequence; intended information or emotional movement | Attractive passages without scene movement, contradictory knowledge, or an unearned ending |

Professional criteria govern adequacy; personal preferences govern choices among adequate alternatives. Use annotated examples when supplied to calibrate why an editorial choice works; do not copy their facts or turn their shape into a universal standard.

For the relevant decisions, connect **input → judgment → usable result → return condition**. For example: source observations → what causal claim is warranted → bounded claim plus supporting material → return to evidence or narrow the claim if support is missing. A header called “立意” does not complete this work.

An upstream result must constrain drafting: each essential passage has a job and material supporting it, or an identified fictional function within creative authorization. Not every source item must appear. If material contradicts the intended claim, revise the claim; if a section has no job, remove or reorganize it within scope. Do not hide either failure under smoother language. If the required repair exceeds authorization, complete permitted edits and identify the remaining issue.

## Integrated Composition At The Requested Length

Separate analytical questions when helpful; keep one editorial responsibility for the complete draft. Fact checks and reader questions may return evidence and findings, not mandatory paragraphs to concatenate. This does not require multiple agents or tool calls.

Design relationships for the available space before writing. Do not default to writing a long essay and deleting its connective reasoning. A sentence may satisfy several needs naturally; it need not name each need. For long work, sections may be drafted separately when their dependencies and roles are clear, followed by an integration pass.

Compare alternative whole approaches only when there is a meaningful unresolved choice. Select one approach; do not collect favorite lines from incompatible alternatives. Preserve useful complexity, necessary qualifications, and genre-appropriate ambiguity. Cohesion does not mean repeating a thesis everywhere or making fiction deliver an explicit lesson.

Illustrative supplied facts: tonight's release is delayed; testing found an export error and repair is underway; the existing version remains usable; the team will report progress tomorrow at 15:00; no new release time is known.

A viable short notice:

> 原定今晚发布的新版本将延期。测试中发现导出异常，我们正在修复，现有版本可继续使用。明天15:00向您同步进展。给您的安排带来不便，非常抱歉。

Never borrow these example facts into a real task. The notice works through the relationship among cause, impact, and expectation. “更安心” cannot turn the progress update into a release promise; “更简短” should preserve the update and current-use information when these are essential to the reader.

## Revision Loop And Acceptance

Use an outer editorial judgment around targeted execution:

1. Identify the current main obstacle to the reader outcome. Distinguish deficient expression from missing material, competing purposes, broken structure, or narrative cause.
2. Choose an authorized intervention and preserve the parts already working. Fix a typo locally; trace a changed claim into its evidence and conclusion; reconsider the complete short text if its emphasis changes.
3. Compare the complete candidate with the last viable draft against the current basis. Identify the concrete gain and any lost fact, qualification, relationship, voice, or effect. Greater length, smoother language, and apparent compliance with the latest adjective are not sufficient gains.
4. Accept a candidate that solves the problem without a more important loss. Otherwise repair the regression or retain the viable wording. Required factual corrections and explicit user changes cannot be rolled back merely to preserve an older version.

When purpose changes, evaluate against the updated basis: do not demand that the old purpose also survive. When feedback remains unmet, retaining a draft is not a claim of completion. Resolve the issue or explain the concrete evidence/scope limitation. Repeated failed rewrites call for revisiting the diagnosis, not an indefinite cycle of alternatives.

## Reader Check And Stop

Read only the finished text with background available to its intended audience; set the author's explanatory notes aside. Describe briefly what the text actually leads a reader to understand, do, expect, or feel. Then compare that reading with the basis. Check misreadings such as “tomorrow's progress report” being read as “tomorrow's fix”, an unexplained comparison, or a scene's emotional turn depending on information never revealed.

This self-review is a proxy, not an independent reader study. Do not claim blind testing or independent validation when the reviewer has already seen the plan. Separate review is optional when available and authorized, not required for every short text.

Finish when hard constraints and the main reader task are met, significant defects are resolved, and remaining changes are preference swaps. Keep a successful version. If professional adequacy still fails, name the actual problem instead of declaring a generic PASS.
